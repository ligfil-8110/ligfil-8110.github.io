## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. Toon-Shaded 3D Figures & Floating Sky Ruins Diorama
Powered by Three.js, every hero and monster renders as a stylized 3D miniature with bold outlines and three-step toon shading.

Your knight, Blue Slimes, Shadow Rogues, Stone Gargoyles, Azure Wizards, and floor bosses animate with jointed strides, wing beats, and full sword swings on every turn.

* A bright **cyan tile outline** highlights your hero's tile, while **crimson-magenta frames** mark monsters clearly.
* Stairs to the next floor stand out immediately as a **recessed stone stairwell flanked by lantern pillars, a rising light column, and a floating 3D golden arrow beacon**, plus a dedicated green stair icon on the minimap.

### 2. Procedural Mystery Dungeon Exploration
Each floor generates 5 to 7 interconnected rooms with corridors, waterways, pillars, items, traps, and occasional Monster Houses.

* **Four Distinct Biomes:**
  * **B1F–B3F:** Sky Cavern
  * **B4F–B6F:** Crimson Lava Cavern (with mid-boss *Infernal Guardian* on B5F)
  * **B7F–B9F:** Azure Star Palace
  * **B10F+:** Astral Sanctum (home to *Zargaus, Star-Eater Demon Lord* on B10F)
* **Classic Roguelike Tools & Traps:** Eat Rice Balls to keep hunger from dropping, recover with herbs, unleash Scrolls of Flame or Vacuum across the whole room, use Bind, Knockback, or Swap staves, and watch out for Landmine, Poison Arrow, Warp, and Sleep Gas traps.

### 3. Sky Sanctum Town, Smithing & Persistent RPG Growth
If your HP hits 0, a red impact flash and dedicated **Game Over result screen** shows your defeat cause, floor reached, enemies slain, and dropped gold (30% of carried gold). Your **Level, SP, and upgraded weapon & shield stay intact**.

At **Sky Sanctum Town**, you can prepare before heading back down:

* **🔨 Smithy:** Spend gold or Forge Gems to upgrade your weapon and shield (`+1` at a time), or forge higher-tier base weapons and shields while carrying over your existing `+` bonus.
* **✨ Skill Upgrades:** Spend SP earned on level-up across 5 skills: *Whirlwind* (8-tile spin attack), *Fireball* (straight ranged spell), *Heal*, *Mind's Eye* (critical hit rate), and *Iron Body* (max HP).
* **💾 Auto-Save & Transfer Code:** Progress saves automatically in `localStorage`, and you can copy or restore a text transfer code anytime.

### 4. 5-Act 3D Ending Movie & Post-Clear Astral Abyss
Defeating *Zargaus, Star-Eater Demon Lord* on B10F triggers a **real-time 5-act 3D ending cutscene** backed by an original Web Audio synthesizer victory theme.

Your equipped weapon and shield colors appear right on your hero inside the movie, followed by rolling credits and a grand clear card. Clearing B10F also unlocks **B11F+ (Astral Abyss)** and lets you replay the ending movie anytime from Town.

## 🕹️ Controls

* **Keyboard:**
  * `W` `A` `S` `D` or `Arrow keys`: Move cardinally / attack adjacent monsters
  * `Q` `E` `Z` `C`: Diagonal move / diagonal attack
  * `Space` / `Enter`: Attack tile in front (press `Enter` on stairs to open the descend prompt)
  * `R`: Wait 1 turn in place / `T`: Change facing direction without moving
  * `1` / `2` / `3`: Whirlwind / Fireball / Heal skills / `I`: Bag & Stats
* **Mouse & Touch:**
  * Click or tap directly on any tile or monster in the 3D scene to step or attack toward it, or use the 8-way D-pad and action buttons at the bottom.

You can also bookmark the permanent [3D Mystery Dungeon RPG page](/en/rpg-3d.html) to launch Astral Labyrinth whenever you want to play.
