### 🕹️ Controls & Features

* **3D Character Models & Intuitive Turn-Based Controls:**
  * The hero, slimes, rogues, gargoyles, wizards, and bosses all move and strike as articulated 3D toon figures.
  * **Keyboard:** Move with `WASD` or `Arrow keys`, move/attack diagonally with `Q` `E` `Z` `C`, strike forward with `Space` / `Enter`, pass 1 turn with `R`, turn without stepping using `T`, cast skills with `1` / `2` / `3`, and open your bag with `I`.
  * **Mouse & Touch:** Click or tap directly on 3D dungeon tiles or monsters to act toward them, or use the on-screen 8-way D-pad and skill buttons.
* **Clear 3D Stairs & Roguelike Depth:**
  * Find the stone stairwell pit marked by lantern pillars, a rising beam of light, a floating golden 3D arrow beacon, and a green stair icon on your minimap.
  * **4 Procedural Biomes:** B1F–B3F *Sky Cavern*, B4F–B6F *Crimson Lava Cavern*, B7F–B9F *Azure Star Palace*, and B10F+ *Astral Sanctum*.
  * **Classic Items & Hazards:** Manage fullness with Rice Balls, heal with herbs, wipe rooms with fire and vacuum scrolls, control enemies with Bind/Knockback/Swap staves, and survive traps and Monster Houses.
* **Sky Sanctum & 5-Act Ending Cutscene:**
  * Even if you fall in battle, you keep your Level, SP, and weapon/shield upgrades when rescued back to Sky Sanctum.
  * Conquer B5F's *Infernal Guardian* and B10F's *Zargaus, Star-Eater Demon Lord* to experience the real-time 5-act 3D ending movie and Web Audio synthesizer score, unlock B11F+ (*Astral Abyss*), and unlock endless ending replays from Town.
