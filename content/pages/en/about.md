## Hi, I’m Ligfil!
I’m also really interested in AI and excited to learn more about what it can do.

This is where I casually write about the things I enjoy, especially games and running.

I share guides and impressions from games I’ve actually played, things I’ve tried with PCs and gadgets I’ve bought, and notes from my runs.

This blog uses Google AdSense and affiliate links. I don’t write as if I’ve used a product when I haven’t, or publish performance claims or experiences I haven’t verified.
